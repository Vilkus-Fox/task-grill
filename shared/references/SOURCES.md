Task Grill introduces development-domain triage, goal traceability, branch-aware question routing, a bounded evidence-search rule, risk-based red-team analysis, staged delivery feasibility and resource-aware handoff. It is tailored to owner-led teams building apps, websites and games; it is not a universal interview skill.
